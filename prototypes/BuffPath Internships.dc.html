Prototype · Round 1

BuffPath

An internship app for CU Boulder students. Merges CU Boulder Handshake postings with public boards, and routes students into Leeds alumni mentor matching when a posting is out of reach.

Assumptions I made
  • Two audiences share one app: first- and second-years with no experience, and juniors hunting a summer role. Class year changes what the daily list is made of, not the navigation.
  • Fit is explained, never scored silently. Every card says which of three signals fired: your listed skills, Leeds alumni inside the company, and CU students hired before.
  • When nothing fits, the app hands back a prep step instead of a weak posting. Prep cards sit in the same daily deck as jobs.
  • Applying means picking one of your saved resume versions, not writing a new one.
Signature interaction

Today's Six. A fixed, finishable daily deck. Swipe or tap right to save, left to pass. Six cards, then the deck is done and the app stops asking for attention.

What I'd do next
  • Interview prep as a real screen, keyed to a specific application in the tracker.
  • An empty-and-early state for a first-year in week one, when there is no skill list to match against yet.
  • Notification rules — what earns a push, and what waits for the daily deck.
